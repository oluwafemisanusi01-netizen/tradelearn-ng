Beginner lessons

01. What is a financial market?

A financial market is a place or system where people and institutions exchange financial assets.

Example: Stock markets allow people to buy and sell shares in companies.

Remember: Financial markets help connect people and organisations that need money with those who have money to invest.

02. Understanding cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrency is a type of digital asset that can use blockchain technology to record transactions.

Example: Bitcoin is a well-known cryptocurrency.

Remember: Cryptocurrency prices can change significantly. Learning about it does not mean you need to buy it.

03. Candlestick charts

A candlestick is a chart symbol that summarises price information over a particular time interval.

Body: Shows the opening and closing prices.

Wick: Shows the highest and lowest prices during that interval.

Remember: A candlestick describes historical price movement; it cannot guarantee what happens next.

04. Market trends

A market trend describes the general direction of prices over a period of time.

Upward: Prices generally move higher.

Downward: Prices generally move lower.

Sideways: Prices move within a relatively limited range.

Remember: Trends describe past or current movement, not guaranteed future results.

05. Financial risk

Financial risk means the possibility that an uncertain event could cause a financial loss.

Example: The value of an asset may fall unexpectedly.

Remember: Understanding risk is an important part of financial education. Never assume an outcome is guaranteed.
